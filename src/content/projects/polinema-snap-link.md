## 1. Website Identity

**Website name:** Polinema SnapLink  
**Website address:** https://poline.ma  
**Institution:** Politeknik Negeri Malang  

Polinema SnapLink is a link-shortening service or *URL shortener* used to make web addresses more concise, easier to share, and officially identified with Politeknik Negeri Malang through the **poline.ma** domain.

---

## 2. Overview

Polinema SnapLink is a digital service designed to support information distribution needs within Politeknik Negeri Malang. This website helps the academic community convert long URLs into shorter links that are simpler and easier to access.

This service can be used to share various types of links, such as forms, documents, attendance lists, surveys, announcements, academic systems, event information, and other campus service pages.

---

## 3. Main Website Functions

The main functions of the Polinema SnapLink website are as follows:

1. Shortening long URLs into more concise links.
2. Making it easier to distribute links to students, lecturers, administrative staff, and related parties.
3. Providing Polinema's official identity to the shared links.
4. Supporting the effectiveness of digital communication within the campus environment.
5. Helping users create links that are easier to remember and more professional.

---

## 4. Target Users

This website is intended for the academic community of Politeknik Negeri Malang, including:

* Lecturers
* Students
* Administrative staff
* Work units
* Study programs
* Departments
* Event committees
* Campus digital service administrators

With the integration of Polinema Email SSO login, the use of this service becomes more controlled and connected to the official campus account ecosystem.
